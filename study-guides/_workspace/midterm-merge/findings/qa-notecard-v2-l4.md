## ⚠️ Inaccuracies (3 items)

### 1. Challacombe Scale — items 9 and 10 SWAPPED in the notecard

**Notecard says (line 226):**
> "Severe 7–10: glassy mucosa · item 9 dental mirror sticks to tongue · item 10 patient sips water during interview"

**Source says (od531-midterm-complete-study-guide.html line 5956–5966 and salivary-gland-disorders-study-guide.html lines 901–910):**
- Item 1 = mirror sticks to buccal mucosa
- Item 2 = mirror sticks to tongue
- Item 9 = cervical caries (>2 teeth) [SEVERE]
- Item 10 = debris on palate or sticking to teeth [SEVERE]

The notecard assigns wrong items to 9 and 10. "Mirror sticks to tongue" is item **2** (MILD), and "patient sips water during interview" appears nowhere in the source's 10-item list — it is not one of the 10 Challacombe features. The correct severe items are cervical caries and palate debris.

**Fix:** Change line 226 to: "item 9 = cervical caries (>2 teeth) · item 10 = debris on palate/teeth"

### 2. Tubarial glands — year should be 2021, not 2020

**Notecard says (line 217):** "Tubarial glands = '4th pair,' 2020 discovery"

**Source (notecard-rebuild-l4.md line 65):** "discovered 2021 (Valstar et al., Radiother Oncol)" — the inventory file explicitly lists 2021. The paper by Valstar MH et al. was published in *Radiotherapy & Oncology* 2021;154:292–298. The discovery/announcement was 2020 (preprint/news cycle), but the citable journal publication is 2021. The source inventory cites 2021.

**Fix:** Change "2020 discovery" to "2021 discovery" OR "2020/2021 discovery (Valstar)" to be safe.

### 3. Polypharmacy threshold — notecard says ≥4, source says >3

**Notecard says (line 229):** "polypharmacy (≥4 meds)"

**Source says (`od531-midterm-complete-study-guide.html` line 5730 and `salivary-gland-disorders-study-guide.html` line 979):** "Use of **>3 medications/day** increases dry mouth risk (OR 2.9)" — the prof and both study guides use >3, not ≥4. These are numerically equivalent (>3 = ≥4), but the source consistently uses the ">3" phrasing. Boards may test the exact wording. Change to ">3 meds/day" to match source verbatim.

## 💡 Improvements

- **SSB claim** — notecard (line 241) says "SSB no longer counts alone." Source still lists SSB as 1 point in the ACR-EULAR 2016 criteria (line 5533). The 2016 criteria DO include anti-SSB at 1 point. The notecard's implication that SSB is removed is misleading. The accurate teaching point is that SSB alone cannot reach ≥4 and that SSA is the more sensitive/weighted marker. Suggest: remove "SSB no longer counts alone" and replace with "SSB=1pt (less sensitive, can't reach ≥4 alone)."
