L1 · INFECTIOUS DISEASES
5-Step Framework (apply to every case)
① Identify lesion (vesicle/ulcer/plaque/papule/crust). ② Locate (oral mucosa vs skin vs both). ③ Determine acuity & systemic sx (fever, lymphadenopathy, malaise). ④ Risk factors (age, sexual hx, immune status, travel, contacts). ⑤ Diagnostic test & targeted tx. Cross-cutting: IgM = current/active · IgG = past/immune memory · "if it wipes off → Candida; if it doesn't → leukoplakia/lichenoid".
Impetigo: honey crusts over lip/perioral skin
Impetigo
MC bacterial skin infxn, 2–5 yr olds. Cause: Staph aureus + β-hemolytic strep. Honey-colored crusts ("cornflakes glued on"). Highly contagious (school outbreaks). Topical: mupirocin 2% TID ×5d. Systemic: Augmentin dose by WEIGHT not age — <40 kg = 25 mg/kg/d BID; ≥40 kg = 500 mg BID. Trap: don't dose by age in pediatrics.
Syphilis (3 stages — match stage to lesion)
Cause: Treponema pallidum — "cursed corkscrews" on bx (IHC preferred 64–94% sens; silver stain old method 0–41%). REPORTABLE all stages. "The Great Imitator". 6-wk serology window — RPR/VDRL may be falsely negative early in 1°.
PRIMARY: painless chancre 3–90 d post-exposure, heals 3–6 wk untreated. SECONDARY (~9 wk): mucous patches ("snail-track ulcers"), maculopapular rash palms+soles, condyloma lata, moth-eaten alopecia. TERTIARY (yrs later): gumma, interstitial glossitis, neuro/CV. CONGENITAL: Hutchinson triad (Hutchinson incisors, mulberry molars, interstitial keratitis), saddle nose, saber shins. Tx: Penicillin G IM (single dose primary; 3-dose tertiary). PCN-allergic: doxycycline.
Tuberculosis (TB)
Cause: Mycobacterium tuberculosis — acid-fast bacillus. REPORTABLE. Primary lung; oral lesion = chronic painful tongue ulcer (rare, posterior dorsal). Dx: PPD ≥10 mm (≥5 mm if HIV/contact), CXR Ghon focus, IGRA (QuantiFERON) if BCG-vaccinated. Tx: RIPE × 2 mo → RI × 4 mo (Rifampin · Isoniazid · Pyrazinamide · Ethambutol). Latent TB: 9 mo INH. Dental: defer elective tx until non-infectious (≥2 wk multidrug + neg sputum).
Candidiasis (6 subtypes)
Cause: Candida albicans. Wipes off = pseudomembranous; doesn't wipe = atrophic/erythematous. ① Pseudomembranous (thrush) — white wipe-off plaques. ② Erythematous/atrophic — red dorsal tongue, post-AB or steroid inhaler. ③ Median rhomboid glossitis — central depapillated rhomboid. ④ Angular cheilitis — corners of mouth, Candida + Staph. ⑤ Chronic hyperplastic — non-wipe-off, can be premalignant. ⑥ Mucocutaneous (CMC). 4 mandatory antifungals: clotrimazole troches (5×/d), nystatin susp (swish/swallow QID), miconazole gel/buccal tab, fluconazole 100–200 mg PO (×7–14d). Clotrimazole troche CI in diabetics (sucrose vehicle → caries/glycemic). Steroid-inhaler pts: rinse + spit after use.
HSV-1 / HSV-2 + Herpesviruses
8 HHVs: HHV-1 HSV-1 · HHV-2 HSV-2 · HHV-3 VZV · HHV-4 EBV · HHV-5 CMV · HHV-6/7 roseola · HHV-8 Kaposi. Primary HSV-1 = acute herpetic gingivostomatitis (ages 6 mo–5 yr): fever, crops of vesicles → ulcers on KERATINIZED + non-keratinized mucosa, dx clinical/Tzanck/PCR. Tx: supportive ± acyclovir 15 mg/kg 5×/d ×7d (start ≤5 d from onset). AVOID viscous lidocaine in children <3 (FDA boxed warning, seizures), avoid ASA in kids (Reye). Recurrent HSV-1 (RHL) = labial vermilion vesicles (cold sore) — prodromal tingling 24h, lasts 7–10d, attached gingiva & hard palate ONLY intraorally (keratinized only, vs aphthous = nonkerat). Tx: topical penciclovir 1% q2h start at 48-h prodrome; severe systemic → valacyclovir 1 g BID ×5d. HSV-2 = anogenital but cross-infect possible.
VZV (HHV-3) — Chickenpox & Shingles
Primary = varicella (chickenpox), recurrent = herpes zoster (shingles) — UNILATERAL, dermatomal, abrupt midline stop. Trigeminal V1 = Hutchinson sign (nose tip vesicle → ophthalmic involvement) — refer ophtho. V2/V3 = oral hemiface. Tx: valacyclovir 1 g TID ×7d if <72 hr. Postherpetic neuralgia common >60. Vaccine: Shingrix ≥50 yr.
CMV (HHV-5)
Owl-eye intranuclear inclusions on bx. #1 nonhereditary cause of SNHL in newborns. Oral = chronic deep punched-out ulcer in immunocompromised (HIV, transplant). Tx: valganciclovir. Pregnancy: TORCH screen.
Measles (Rubeola)
REPORTABLE → MA DPH. Koplik spots (blue-white grains opp upper molars) precede skin rash by 1–2 d = pathognomonic. 3 C's: Cough · Coryza · Conjunctivitis. Cephalocaudal rash. Airborne isolation until 4d after rash. MMR vaccine. Recent US outbreaks → high-yield.
Mumps
Paramyxovirus. Bilateral parotitis + fever + orchitis. 10–30% have NO parotid swelling (trap — don't rule out). Dx: RT-PCR buccal swab + IgM. Saliva test > serology in vaccinated. Reportable. MMR vaccine.
EBV (HHV-4) & EBV-MCU
Mono ("kissing dz"): fever + posterior cervical LAD + pharyngitis + splenomegaly. Avoid amox/ampicillin → maculopapular rash. EBV-MCU (mucocutaneous ulcer) = solitary indurated ulcer in immunosenescent/iatrogenic (elderly, MTX, biologics). DDx vs SCC vs lymphoma. Dx: EBER ISH. Discriminators (vs lymphoma): normal LDH · normal IL-2R · EBV DNA <1,000 copies/mL. Often self-resolves with immunosuppressant taper.
L1 Cross-cutting Traps
"Wipes off" rule → Candida pseudo (DDx: strep, herpangina); doesn't wipe → hyperplastic Candida (bx) OR leukoplakia/lichenoid. Keratinized vs non-kerat → recurrent HSV = kerat ONLY (att ging, hard palate); aphthous = non-kerat ONLY. Reportable: syphilis (all stages), TB, measles, mumps, HIV/AIDS → MA DPH. Augmentin = weight-based. Penicillin G = syphilis gold std. Antiviral windows: PrHGS acyclovir ≤5 d · RHL penciclovir at 48-h prodrome · zoster valacyclovir <72 h. No ASA <18, no viscous lidocaine <3. Mumps IgM may be falsely neg in vaccinated — RT-PCR is gold std. Hutchinson triad = congenital syphilis ≠ Hutchinson nasal-tip sign = V1 zoster. TB: never bx first — workup + airborne PPE + ID consult.
L2 · ALLERGIES & IMMUNOLOGIC
Hypersensitivity (4 types + pseudoallergic)
Type I (immediate, IgE) — anaphylaxis, urticaria, angioedema. Mast cell + histamine. Tx: epi 0.3 mg IM lat thigh. Type II (cytotoxic, IgG/IgM vs cell Ag) — pemphigus, MMP, hemolytic anemia. Type III (immune complex) — SLE, vasculitis, serum sickness. Type IV (delayed, T-cell, 24–72 h) — contact dermatitis, OLP, drug rxns, TB skin test. Pseudoallergic (non-IgE) — radiocontrast, NSAIDs, opioids → direct mast cell.
RAS (Recurrent Aphthous Stomatitis)
Non-keratinized mucosa ONLY (vs RHL keratinized). Yellow-gray pseudomembrane + erythematous halo. 3 variants: Minor (<1 cm, 7–10d, no scar, MC 80%) · Major (Sutton) (>1 cm, weeks-months, scars) · Herpetiform (50–100 pinpoint, coalesce). Complex aphthosis = ≥3 simultaneous OR continuous OR genital. Onset >40 = workup (Behçet, IBD, celiac, HIV, hematinic def — B12/folate/Fe/zinc). Tx ladder: topical clobetasol 0.05% > fluocinonide 0.05% > dex elixir 0.5 mg/5 mL swish/spit BID > triamcinolone in Orabase (trap — least potent). Severe: PO prednisone, colchicine, dapsone, apremilast.
Corticosteroid Pharmacotherapy (memorize ladder)
Ultra-high: clobetasol 0.05%. High: fluocinonide 0.05%. Dex elixir 0.5 mg/5 mL — universal first-line swish. Low (TRAP): triamcinolone in Orabase = weakest, do not lead with. Rules: vermilion border NEVER (atrophy/telangiectasia). Sensodyne SLS trap — SLS aggravates RAS, recommend SLS-free toothpastes: Cleure, Dr. Bob, Sensodyne ProNamel SLS-free, Biotene. Dex elixir Rx: 0.5 mg/5 mL, swish 5 mL ×2 min spit BID-QID ×2 wk.
Geographic Tongue (Benign Migratory Glossitis)
3 D's: Depapillated red patches · Demarcated white serpiginous border · Dynamic (migrates over days — rules out malignancy). Dorsal/lateral tongue. Type IV-like. Asymptomatic; some burning to acidic foods. Reassure. Variant: ectopic geographic stomatitis (extra-tongue). Assoc psoriasis.
Drug-Related Gingival Hyperplasia (DRGH)
3 drug classes: Phenytoin · Ca-channel blockers (nifedipine, amlodipine) · Cyclosporine. Onset 1–3 mo after start. Anterior labial gingiva first. Tx: meticulous plaque control ± gingivectomy ± drug switch (consult MD).
Transient Lingual Papillitis (TLP) — "lie bumps"
Painful red/white papules on fungiform papillae (tip + dorsolateral tongue). Resolves 2–15 days. Tx: reassurance first-line; bland diet, topical anesthetic if severe.
Plasma Cell Gingivitis & Cinnamomum Reaction (PCG)
Cinnamon flavoring (toothpaste, gum, candy) → cinnamomum reaction: lateral tongue + buccal mucosa erythema/erosion. Plasma cell gingivitis = bright red, sharply demarcated free + att gingiva. Tx: eliminate cinnamon.
Immunobullous Framework — Pegasus & Hades
Pegasus (intraepithelial, suprabasilar) = PV (DSG3/DSG1, IgG, "row of tombstones," chicken-wire DIF). Hades (subepithelial, BMZ) = MMP, BP, EBA, LABD (linear DIF along BMZ). Nikolsky sign: positive in PV, SJS/TEN, PNP; negative in MMP/BP (subepithelial tense roof), OLP, RAS, EM. DIF: PV chicken-wire intercellular IgG · MMP linear IgG/C3 BMZ · OLP shaggy fibrinogen BMZ.
OLP (Oral Lichen Planus)
Auto-INFLAMMATORY, NOT autoimmune (T-cell mediated). MC reticular (Wickham striae bilateral buccal); also erosive, plaque, atrophic, papular, bullous. 4 P's: Pruritic · Purple · Polygonal · Papules (skin). Wickham striae = white lacy bilateral buccal. OPMD controversy — ~1.4% MT risk (WHO classifies as OPMD; debated). Bx for atypical/erosive. Tx: clobetasol/fluocinonide; dex elixir; tacrolimus 0.1% if refractory.
MMP (Mucous Membrane Pemphigoid)
Subepithelial split, BMZ. Nikolsky NEGATIVE (tense subepithelial blister, thick roof). Desquamative gingivitis = #1 oral presentation. REFER ALL TO OPHTHALMOLOGY (symblepharon → blindness). Anti-laminin 332+ MMP → 25% occult adenocarcinoma — cancer screen mandatory. Biopsy 2-vial trap: perilesional (intact mucosa, NOT ulcer center) for DIF in Michel's medium; second specimen in formalin for H&E. "Don't Lose Your Sushi" = Don't bx ulcer Lesion, Use 2 vials, Yes perilesional, Submit Michel's. Tx: clobetasol/dex; refractory → dapsone, MMF, rituximab.
Pemphigus Vulgaris (PV)
IgG vs DSG3 (oral) ± DSG1 (skin). Acantholysis → "row of tombstones" basal cells. >50% present orally FIRST (months before skin). Painful flaccid bullae → erosions, Nikolsky+. DIF chicken-wire intercellular IgG. Severity: PDAI score. Tx: high-dose pred ± rituximab, MMF, AZA, IVIG. Untreated mortality high.
EM / RIME vs SJS / TEN
EM: target lesions, ~70% HSV-triggered (EM/RIME combined infection-triggered ~90%); EM minor <10% BSA + ≤1 mucosa, EM major ≥2 mucosa. RIME = post-infectious mucositis (Mycoplasma, COVID). SJS <10% BSA, SJS/TEN overlap 10–30%, TEN >30% BSA. Triggers: drugs >90% — allopurinol #1, sulfonamides, anticonvulsants (carbamazepine, lamotrigine, phenytoin), NSAIDs (oxicams). HLA-B*1502 + carbamazepine in Han Chinese/SE Asian → screen. SCORTEN mortality. Refer burn unit.
PNP / PAMS (Paraneoplastic Pemphigus)
Always = malignancy: NHL, CLL, Castleman dz, thymoma, Waldenström. Severe intractable stomatitis + polymorphous skin. Anti-plakin Ab (envoplakin, periplakin). Bronchiolitis obliterans → respiratory failure = leading cause of death. Tx: treat underlying tumor + immunosuppression.
L2 Cross-cutting Tables (high-yield)
Nikolsky+: PV, SJS/TEN, PNP. Nikolsky−: MMP/BP, OLP, RAS, EM. DIF chicken-wire = PV; linear BMZ = MMP/BP; shaggy fibrinogen = OLP; granular IgA dermal papillae = DH. BSA cutoffs: SJS <10 · overlap 10–30 · TEN >30. Histology: PV intraepithelial suprabasilar; MMP/BP subepithelial; OLP saw-tooth rete + band-like lymphocytes; EM apoptotic keratinocytes. Drug allergy: PCN cross-react cephalosporins ~1%; sulfa cross-react furosemide; NSAID cross-react ASA. Contact stomatitis: nickel, chromium, gold, mercury, balsam of Peru, cinnamaldehyde, eugenol. IBS vs IBD trap: IBS no oral findings; IBD (Crohn) = cobblestone, lip swelling, OFG, pyostomatitis vegetans, RAS-like.

L3 · EPITHELIAL PATHOSES & SCC
Foundations
Pathosis = the disease/lesion; pathology = the study. WHO 2017: OPMD = oral potentially malignant disorder (replaces "premalignant"). Dysplasia = architectural + cytologic atypia confined above BM; CIS = full-thickness atypia; SCC = invasion through BM. Visual exam: ~60% sens, ~62% spec — bx is gold std.
Epi / Risk
US 2024: ~36,600 new oral/oropharyngeal cancers, ~12,230 deaths. M:F 2–4:1. Tongue (lateral) = 41.7% oral cavity site. Risk: tobacco × alcohol = 30× multiplicative (not additive). Fanconi anemia OR 40.6×. Other: betel/areca, immunosuppression, prior cancer, UV (lip vermilion). Lip vermilion = separate dz (UV-driven, lower lip).
HPV Benign Lesions (don't confuse with SCC)
Squamous papilloma — HPV 6, 11, single cauliflower. Condyloma acuminatum — HPV 6, 11, STI, multiple. Verruca vulgaris — HPV 2, 4, autoinoc fingers→lip. Heck disease (focal epithelial hyperplasia) — HPV 13, 32, multiple soft pink papules, NOT covered by Gardasil 9 (TRAP). Gardasil 9 = HPV 6/11/16/18/31/33/45/52/58.
Leukoplakia & Malignant Transformation
Definition: CLINICAL term, not histologic — white plaque that cannot be wiped or scraped (unlike pseudomembranous Candida); bx returns "hyperkeratosis," never "leukoplakia." MT by morphology: homogeneous ~2% · non-homogeneous 15–25% · verrucous/PVL 29–50%. MT by dysplasia grade (separate stat): no dysp 2.2% · mild 2.6–11.9% · mod 4.1–8.7% · severe/CIS 29.2–32.2%. 39.6% paradox: 39.6% of leukoplakias that progressed to SCC had NO dysplasia on initial biopsy. Bx every clinically suspicious lesion.
Erythroplakia (highest single MT)
Velvety red plaque. ~50% MT. 75–90% already dysplasia/CIS/SCC at biopsy. Mean 3.7 mo from dx → cancer. Floor of mouth, soft palate, ventral tongue = highest-risk sites. Bx always, urgently.
Proliferative Verrucous Leukoplakia (PVL)
48–50% MT, ~8× lifetime risk vs leukoplakia. F > M, often non-smokers. Gingiva "ring around the collar" = signature. Multifocal, recurrent despite tx. Cerero/Villa criteria. Long-term surveillance — recurrence rule, not exception.
OPMD ranking (highest→lowest MT)
① PVL (~48–50%, highest of any OPMD) ② Erythroplakia (~50% but smaller cohort) ③ Erythroleukoplakia ④ OSF (~5.1%) ⑤ Verrucous leukoplakia ⑥ Non-homogeneous leukoplakia ⑦ Homogeneous leukoplakia ⑧ Actinic cheilitis ⑨ OLP (1.4%, debated OPMD).
Oral Submucous Fibrosis (OSF)
Areca/betel nut chewers (S Asian). Trismus, blanched mucosa, fibrous bands buccal. 5.1% MT, mean 36 mo. IRREVERSIBLE once established. EMT cascade drives fibrosis + transformation. Stop nut, intralesional steroids/hyaluronidase, mouth-opening exercises.
Biopsy Rules (memorize)
"Red over white, edge over center, deep for verrucous, multiple for big." Sample most clinically alarming area (red > white, indurated). Edge = perilesional includes normal-abnormal junction. Verrucous = deep wedge (surface bx misses invasion). Lesions >1.5 cm → ≥2 sites. Tumor seeding from biopsy = MYTH (do not delay bx for fear of seeding).
Dysplasia Management
Mild → observe + risk-factor mod ± laser. Moderate–severe/CIS → excision. Modalities: scalpel OR 3.6, CO2 laser HR 0.14 (best recurrence ↓), diode 93.5% CR, cryotherapy 100% CR, ALA-PDT 66–100% CR. Re-bx if recur.
Chemoprevention (background only)
Imiquimod (TLR-7 agonist) — topical, mucosal trials. Beta-carotene CARET trap: ↑lung cancer in smokers, do NOT use. RP2 trial (BWH/Dana-Farber) — ongoing.
Oral SCC
>90% of oral malignancies. Mean age 66. M:F 2–4:1. Site MC lateral tongue 42% > FOM > soft palate. Induration = #1 sign. 5 warning signs: ulcer >2 wk · induration · fixed lymph node · pain/dysesthesia · loose tooth/poorly fitting denture. Bx mandatory if persistent.
AJCC 8th — what changed
DOI added to T-staging: T1 ≤2 cm AND DOI ≤5 mm · T2 ≤4 cm AND DOI ≤10 mm · T3 >4 cm OR DOI >10 mm · T4 invades adj structures. ENE added to N-stage: ENE+ upstages. N1 single ipsi ≤3 cm ENE− · N2a single ipsi 3–6 cm ENE− OR ≤3 cm ENE+ · N3b ENE+. Stage I → IVC. DOI = depth of invasion from BM, NOT tumor thickness.
HPV-OPSCC (oropharynx only)
Tonsil + base of tongue. HPV-16 in ~90%. 5-yr OS HPV+ ~80–85% vs HPV− ~40%. p16 IHC = surrogate for HPV — reliable in oropharynx ONLY, NOT oral cavity. E6 → p53 inactivation; E7 → Rb inactivation. Gardasil 9 covers 9 types (6/11/16/18/31/33/45/52/58). M>F, oral sex, younger pts.
Carcinogenesis Cascade
Normal → hyperplasia → mild → moderate → severe dysplasia → CIS → invasive SCC. Field cancerization = whole upper aerodigestive carcinogen-exposed; 2nd primary risk 3–7%/yr. ~17% of OPMD progress; remainder regress or stable.
Verrucous Carcinoma
Low-grade SCC variant. White cauliflower, slow, 0% metastasis. Wide pushing border (NOT infiltrative — that's classic SCC). Deep wedge bx mandatory — superficial bx misses dx (calls it "verrucous hyperplasia"). Surgical excision; avoid RT (anaplastic transformation risk).
Oral Melanoma
~50% on hard palate. UV NOT cause (intraoral). ~15% amelanotic (NOT 25%) — pink/red, easy miss. Mol: c-KIT mutations → imatinib; BRAF mutations rare (vs cutaneous). Poor prognosis, often advanced at dx. Bx any pigmented oral lesion that is new, growing, multifocal, or asymmetric.
L4 · SALIVARY GLAND DISORDERS
Anatomy & Output
3 majors: parotid (serous, Stensen duct opp upper 2nd molar), submandibular (mixed, Wharton duct floor of mouth, ~70% resting saliva), sublingual (mucous, Rivinus ducts). 600–1000 minor glands (lip, palate, buccal). Tubarial glands = "4th pair," Valstar 2021 (Radiother Oncol), torus tubarius. Waterfall mnemonic: parotid → over masseter → through buccinator → Stensen at upper 2M (everything flows down).
10 Functions of Saliva
① Lubrication ② Antimicrobial (lysozyme, lactoferrin, IgA) ③ Buffering (bicarbonate) ④ Remineralization (Ca, PO4, F) ⑤ Digestion (amylase) ⑥ Taste solubilization ⑦ Mucosal protection (mucins) ⑧ Speech/swallowing ⑨ Hormone barrier (kallikrein) ⑩ Wound healing (EGF, NGF).
Hyposalivation vs Xerostomia (different!)
Hyposalivation = OBJECTIVE flow ↓. Xerostomia = SUBJECTIVE dry-mouth complaint. Cutoffs: unstim ≤0.1 mL/min · stim ≤0.5–0.7 mL/min. Normal unstim 0.3–0.4, stim 1–2. ~16–27% overlap (some xerostomia w/ normal flow; some hyposal w/o complaint).
Challacombe Scale (10 items, score 1–10)
Mild 1–3: mirror sticks to mucosa (item 2) · loss of palatal rugae · frothy saliva. Moderate 4–6: tongue lobulated/fissured · altered gingival architecture · glassy mucosa appearance. Severe 7–10: active dental decay · debris on palate / sticking to teeth · item 9 cervical caries (>2 teeth) · item 10 debris on palate or sticking to teeth.
Causes (ranked)
Top 3: anxiety · polypharmacy >3 meds/day (OR 2.9) · dehydration. Then: anticholinergics, antihistamines, antidepressants (TCAs, SSRIs), antihypertensives, opioids, diuretics, chemo, head/neck RT, Sjögren, diabetes (uncontrolled), HIV, hepatitis C. OR table: anticholinergics ~3–4×, polypharmacy ~2.9×.
Radiation Caries (head/neck RT)
6 features: cervical & incisal edges · rapid progression · circumferential · brown-black · pulp involvement early · symmetric. Thresholds: parotid >25 Gy → permanent xerostomia; submandibular >39 Gy; >65–70 Gy → ORN risk. Tx: F⁻ trays 1.1% NaF nightly, CPP-ACP, sialagogues, fastidious recall q3mo, pre-RT extractions.
Sialagogues — M3 Pharmacology
Pilocarpine — non-selective M-agonist, 5 mg PO TID-QID. Cevimeline — M1+M3, 30 mg TID. Bethanechol — M2+M3 (off-label). CIs: narrow-angle glaucoma, uncontrolled asthma, severe COPD/CHF, cardiac disease, GI/GU obstruction. SE: sweating, GI cramps, urinary urgency, miosis, bradycardia. Ramadan dosing: pilocarpine pre-suhoor + post-iftar to comply with fasting.
Avoid / Use Rules (DIE ON THIS HILL)
Vaseline = NO on lips (petrolatum sits on surface, no hydration, dries the dry). Lanolin (HPA-Lanolin) = YES — biomimetic. Avoid alcohol mouthwash (Listerine red), avoid SLS. Use: Biotene, Xylimelts at bedtime, OraCoat, ACT dry mouth, sip water, sugar-free gum (xylitol). Humidifier at night.
Sjögren Syndrome (ACR-EULAR 2016)
Score ≥4 = classification. Items: labial salivary gland bx focus score ≥1 (=3 pts), anti-SSA/Ro+ (=3), ocular staining ≥5 (=1), Schirmer ≤5 mm/5 min (=1), unstim flow ≤0.1 mL/min (=1), SSB+ (=1, cannot reach ≥4 alone). Exclusions: HCV, HIV, sarcoid, amyloid, GVHD, IgG4-RD, head/neck RT. ~30% seronegative. F:M 9:1. Primary vs secondary (other CTD). 5–10% lifetime MALT lymphoma risk, 7–19× RR. Red flags: persistent unilateral parotid swelling, fixed mass, B sx, ↑LDH, low C4. Bilateral fluctuant = reassuring.
Sialolithiasis (Stones)
Submandibular 80–90% (Wharton, mucous, alkaline, gravity). Meal-time pain & swelling = pathognomonic ("mealtime syndrome"). 3 mechanisms: salivary stasis · sialomicrolith nidus · ductal inflammation. Occlusal radiograph FIRST; CT if non-radiopaque. Sialendoscopy 85–92% success; bimanual milking; conservative tx for small distal stones.
Bacterial Sialadenitis
S. aureus #1; also strep, anaerobes. Risk: dehydration, post-op, hyposalivation. Acute parotid swelling, pus from Stensen on milking. Tx: Augmentin OR clindamycin, hydration, sialagogues, warm compresses, gland massage. ED criteria: airway compromise, sepsis, abscess.
Mucocele & Ranula
Mucocele: lower lip 60–70%, lip-biting trauma. Extravasation (no epithelial lining, MC) vs retention (lined, less common). Bluish dome, fluctuant, recurrent. Tx: excise WITH feeding minor gland (otherwise recurs). Ranula = FOM mucocele from sublingual gland; "frog belly." Plunging ranula dissects through mylohyoid → neck mass. Tx: marsupialization or sublingual gland excision (definitive).
Salivary Gland Tumors (gland-size paradox)
Larger gland = more benign; smaller gland = more malignant. Parotid ~80% benign; submand ~50/50; sublingual + minor >80% malignant. Pleomorphic adenoma = MC benign (parotid tail), painless mobile mass, low MT to carcinoma-ex-PA. Warthin tumor = papillary cystadenoma lymphomatosum, smokers, M>F, often bilateral parotid. Mucoepidermoid carcinoma = MC malignant, palate in minor glands, low/intermediate/high grade. Adenoid cystic = perineural invasion, late mets. FNA + MRI for any persistent salivary mass.

ORAL = FIRST SIGN (don't miss these)
Pemphigus Vulgaris — >50% present orally first, months before skin. Painful flaccid bullae, Nikolsky+.
MMP — desquamative gingivitis often only finding for years. REFER OPHTHO all cases (symblepharon → blindness).
Crohn disease (IBD) — oral cobblestone, lip swelling, OFG, pyostomatitis vegetans, RAS-like ulcers can precede GI sx.

AVOID RULES (exam traps disguised as common practice)
Vermilion border NEVER apply potent steroids (clobetasol/fluocinonide) → atrophy/telangiectasia.
Vaseline on lips = NO in xerostomia (occlusive only, no biomimicry). Use HPA-Lanolin YES.
Sensodyne with SLS aggravates RAS — switch to SLS-free Sensodyne ProNamel / Biotene.
Listerine alcohol in xerostomia — drying. Use alcohol-free (Biotene rinse, ACT dry mouth).
Triamcinolone in Orabase = weakest, do not lead with — exam expects clobetasol/dex elixir.
Clotrimazole troche CI in diabetics (sucrose vehicle).
Amoxicillin/ampicillin in mono (EBV) → near-100% maculopapular rash.
ASA in children <18 with viral illness → Reye syndrome.
Viscous lidocaine in children <3 → FDA boxed warning, seizures/death.
Beta-carotene in smokers (CARET trial) → ↑lung cancer.
Carbamazepine in Han Chinese / SE Asian without HLA-B*1502 screen → SJS/TEN.
RT to verrucous CA → anaplastic transformation; surgical excision instead.
Sialagogues in narrow-angle glaucoma, uncontrolled asthma, severe COPD/CHF, cardiac disease, GI/GU obstruction → CI.
Bx ulcer center alone for immunobullous → false negative DIF; sample perilesional in Michel's.
Tumor seeding from biopsy = MYTH; never delay biopsy of suspicious oral lesion.
p16 IHC in oral cavity SCC = unreliable; only valid in oropharynx.
Empiric AB before bx of bacterial sialadenitis if no culture? — clinical (S. aureus most likely) is fine; abscess drain.

DDx MINI-CARDS (presentation → top differentials)
Painless ulcer >2 wk → SCC #1 · primary syphilitic chancre · TB ulcer · NHL/EBV-MCU · deep fungal · traumatic ulcer (rule out by removing irritant). Bx mandatory if persists.

Painful necrotic deep ulcer in immunocompromised → CMV · EBV-MCU · deep fungal (mucor, histo) · NUG/NUP · lymphoma · vasculitis (GPA). Bx + cultures + EBER + viral PCR.

White plaque "wipes off" → pseudomembranous candidiasis (clotrim/nystatin/flucon). Doesn't wipe → leukoplakia, OLP, frictional, hairy leukoplakia (EBV in HIV), white sponge nevus, leukoedema.

Multiple oral vesicles in child <5 → primary HSV gingivostomatitis · HFMD (Coxsackie A) · varicella · enteroviral herpangina (soft palate only).

Unilateral persistent parotid mass → pleomorphic adenoma · Warthin (smoker) · mucoepidermoid CA · adenoid cystic CA (perineural pain) · MALT lymphoma in Sjögren · metastasis. FNA + MRI.

Desquamative gingivitis → MMP > OLP > PV > LABD > chronic ulcerative stomatitis. Perilesional bx + DIF.

Target lesions + lip crusts → EM (HSV) · RIME · SJS overlap (BSA, drugs). Drug + BSA >10% = SJS, refer.
